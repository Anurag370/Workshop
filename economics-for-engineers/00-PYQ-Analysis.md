## 5. One-mark items actually asked (with answers)

### 2022-2023 (Group A)
| Item | Question (condensed) | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| I | How does inflation help in the redistribution of income? | It shifts purchasing power from fixed-income earners (savers, lenders) to borrowers and asset-holders, because money loses value |
| II | Market value > book value ⇒ depreciation not charged (T/F) | **False** — depreciation is based on cost and useful life, not market value |
| III | What is a cash-flow diagram? | A time-scaled diagram showing cash inflows (up) and outflows (down) against time |
| IV | Cash-flows method used by IRR and NPV is known as ____ | **Discounted cash-flow (DCF) technique** |
| V | What is inflation? | A sustained rise in the general price level, i.e. a fall in purchasing power of money |
| VI | Adequate maintenance ⇒ no depreciation needed (T/F) | **False** — maintenance preserves condition; depreciation is still charged |
| VII | TVM: money today is more valuable than money in future (T/F) | **True** |
| VIII | Cash flow as investing activity = "Sale of Investment by Non-Financial Enterprise" (T/F) | **True** |
| IX | Minimum expected return needed to persuade an investor = rate of return (T/F) | **True** (it is the minimum acceptable / hurdle rate of return) |
| X | Main objective of depreciation is calculation of net profit (T/F) | **False** — its purpose is cost allocation, tax computation, replacement funding and fair profit measurement |
| XI | What is working capital? | Current assets minus current liabilities; the short-term liquidity of a business |
| XII | Risk–return correlation over a longer period is highly positive (T/F) | **False** — higher risk must be matched by higher expected return, but the relation is weak, not highly positive |

### 2023-2024 (Group A)
| Item | Question (condensed) | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| I | Stagnation + inflation = ____ | **Stagflation** |
| II | In bankruptcy, stock above common stock and below debt = ____ | **Preference stock** |
| III | What is WPI? | Wholesale Price Index — price index of goods traded in bulk at wholesale level |
| IV | Sensitivity of demand to change in price = ____ | **Price elasticity of demand** |
| V | Decision tree algorithm belongs to the ____ family | **Supervised learning** family of machine learning |
| VI | Buying goods without paying suppliers immediately is recorded as ____ | **Trade payable (accounts payable / creditor)** — a current liability |
| VII | Usage of the power-sizing model? | To estimate the cost of a larger or smaller capacity plant from a known base plant |
| VIII | Debenture holder is qualified for fixed-rate interest (T/F) | **True** |
| IX | Two risky securities can be turned riskless if ____ | **They are perfectly negatively correlated (diversified)** |
| X | Accruals, notes payable, accounts payable listed under current liabilities (T/F) | **True** |
| XI | Issuing stocks and bonds to raise funds results in ____ | A **change in the capital structure / financial leverage** |
| XII | What is the BCR? | Ratio of present worth of benefits to present worth of costs; BCR > 1 means the project is acceptable |

## 7. Unclear / flagged items

1. **2024-2025 Group A item VII** — the PDF prints "-------------------- beer describes variable cost ?".
   The wording is corrupted. It is *not* used as a question anywhere in these notes; only a generic
   variable-cost item is practised.
2. **2023-2024 C-Q9(a)(iii) "What are the federal income taxes for the year?"** — the paper gives no
   tax rate. Notes in [Chapter 04](04-Depreciation.md) give the general form
   (tax = taxable income × applicable rate) and show the working with 35% clearly labelled as an
   *assumed textbook rate*, not as data from the paper.
3. **2023-2024 Q8(a)** — "Identify i, f and i′" is clear, but the paper does not state whether the
   5.5% rate is nominal or effective for a one-year deposit; it is compounded annually, so $i = 5.5\%$
   and $i' = (1+i)/(1+f)-1$ is used.
4. **2024-2025 C-Q11** — the printed trial balance balances (Rs 71,900 both sides) with the readings
   used here: Capital 42,000; Creditors 12,000; Bills Payable 6,400; Wages Outstanding 500;
   Provision for Doubtful Debts 1,000; Gross Profit 10,000. If a source types Gross Profit as
   19,000, the trial balance will not agree — use 10,000.
5. **2024-2025 Group B Q2** — "power sizing factor 0.80" is used as the exponent $x$ in
   $C_2 = C_1(Q_2/Q_1)^x$, which is the standard reading.
